How it works & tips

Each heartbeat pushes blood through the skin, subtly changing how much light it reflects. We average the color of your forehead and cheeks across video frames, isolate the pulse with the POS (Plane-Orthogonal-to-Skin) algorithm, and find its frequency to get beats-per-minute.

  • Sit still and face even, bright light (avoid backlight).
  • Give it ~10 seconds to warm up.
  • It's a fun estimate — not a medical device.

🔒 All processing happens locally in your browser. No video, image, or measurement is ever uploaded.

Method: remote photoplethysmography (rPPG), POS — Wang et al., IEEE TBME 2017. Face tracking: MediaPipe FaceLandmarker.
